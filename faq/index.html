Frequently asked questions

Direct answers about SkyPSI cleaning services.

These answers describe the public service approach. Property-specific scope, availability, insurance documentation, procurement, and final pricing are confirmed through review.

What does SkyPSI clean?

SkyPSI focuses on exterior windows and architectural glass; interior windows, doors, partitions, mirrors, and skylights; frames, sills, screens, and tracks; specialty glass restoration; suitable pressure and soft washing; gutters and guards; solar panels; balcony and railing glass; and related exterior-building cleaning.

Is SkyPSI only a drone cleaning company?

No. Drones are one option. Depending on the property, the plan may use robotics, purified-water and water-fed systems, pressure or soft washing, traditional interior tools, specialty restoration, or a hybrid approach.

How does SkyPSI select a cleaning method?

Method selection considers surfaces, condition, height, access, occupants, operating hours, water and runoff, surrounding property, weather, and the desired finish. The property-specific proposal records the planned approach.

Can SkyPSI clean interior and exterior glass together?

Yes. Interior and exterior glass, detail components, and related exterior services can be coordinated in one property-specific scope when that is the right fit.

Does SkyPSI perform hard-water or mineral restoration?

SkyPSI can assess hard-water and mineral deposits, bird droppings, and other specialty glass conditions. Restoration suitability depends on the glass, coating, damage, and test results.

Who performs the work?

SkyPSI is the accountable customer-facing service contractor. It coordinates qualified field capability, which may include specialty cleaning businesses, around the accepted scope, methods, and quality requirements.

Can Mack give me a rough estimate?

Mack may give rough planning math after collecting enough scale and scope information and verifying a business email. It is not a quote. Final scope and price remain property-specific.

Where does SkyPSI currently operate?

Dallas-Fort Worth is the current density focus. Other Texas locations are reviewed against scope and qualified capacity without an automatic serviceability promise.

What happens after I contact SkyPSI?

Mack collects the initial property and contact details. The team then determines whether more photos, a site assessment, technical review, or procurement information is needed before a proposal.
