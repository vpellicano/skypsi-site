A useful first handoff

Bring the basics. Add detail as the conversation earns it.

  • 01
    Property

    Address, building type, and number of stories.

  • 02
    Scope

    Interior or exterior glass, washing, restoration, gutters, solar panels, or related exterior care.

  • 03
    Scale

    Approximate square footage, window count, elevations, and access conditions if known.

  • 04
    Work identity

    Name, company, and business email. Personal or free-mail addresses cannot unlock a rough planning estimate.
