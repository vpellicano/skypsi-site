How it works

From property question to documented closeout.

SkyPSI remains the accountable customer-facing service contractor while coordinating the qualified people, tools, and specialty capability the job requires.

  1. 01

    Share the property

    Tell Mack what needs attention, where it is, and what makes access or timing difficult.

  2. 02

    Define the scope

    We assess surfaces, access, surrounding conditions, operating windows, and the finish standard.

  3. 03

    Select the method

    The plan may use drones, robotics, water-fed systems, pressure or soft washing, traditional tools, restoration, or a hybrid.

  4. 04

    Deliver and document

    The property-specific proposal defines scope and acceptance before work begins; closeout records what was completed.
