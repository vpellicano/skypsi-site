Service scope

One service family, tailored to the surfaces on site.

A site may need routine cleaning, detailed component care, restoration, or coordinated interior and exterior work. The final scope stays specific to the property.

01

Exterior windows and architectural glass

Cleaning plans for storefronts, offices, multifamily properties, hospitality, senior living, and other commercial buildings.

02

Interior glass

Windows, glass doors, partitions, mirrors, skylights, balcony glass, and railing glass, planned around occupants and operating hours.

03

Detailed window care

Frames, sills, screens, and tracks can be included so the delivered scope matches the condition of the full opening.

04

Specialty glass restoration

Assessment and treatment options for hard-water and mineral deposits, bird droppings, and other glass conditions that need more than routine cleaning.

05

Pressure and soft washing

Pressure washing for suitable flatwork and hard surfaces, plus soft washing for suitable building exteriors, siding, and roofs.

06

Exterior-building care

Gutters and guards, solar panels, and related exterior-building cleaning coordinated as one property-specific scope.

Method selection

The tool follows the job.

SkyPSI evaluates height, facade geometry, surface condition, water and runoff, occupants, operating hours, surrounding property, and weather before selecting a method.

DR

Drones

Evaluated for suitable exterior access and surface conditions.

RB

Robotics

Considered when controlled, repeatable surface work fits the property.

PW

Purified water

Water-fed systems can support detailed exterior glass cleaning where appropriate.

HY

Hybrid delivery

Pressure, soft-wash, traditional interior tools, restoration, and modern systems can be combined around the actual scope.

Where technology can fit

Suitable exterior access, repeatable facade work, purified-water cleaning, visual planning, documentation, and controlled property workflows.

Where hands-on work stays essential

Interior glass, detail work, tracks and screens, specialty restoration, tight edges, sensitive finishes, and conditions that require direct judgment.

Why hybrid plans matter

A single property can call for different approaches by elevation, surface, occupancy, or condition. The proposal defines the combination before delivery.

Assessment

What we need to build a useful plan.

Start with the address and the cleaning problem. Helpful context includes approximate glass or surface area, number of stories, access restrictions, desired timing, and current condition.

  • Property address and type
  • Exterior and interior surfaces
  • Approximate size or window count
  • Stories and access conditions
  • Operating and occupant constraints
  • Water, runoff, weather, and surrounding-property considerations
  • Restoration conditions or recurring buildup
  • Timing, procurement, and documentation needs
